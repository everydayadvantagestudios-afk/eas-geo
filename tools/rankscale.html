What Rankscale does

Rankscale is an AI search visibility platform focused on tracking how brands appear in AI-generated answers across major engines. Its core functionality centers on prompt-based citation monitoring, you define the queries relevant to your business, and Rankscale tracks which brands those queries surface across AI engines on a scheduled basis.

The competitive intelligence layer is what distinguishes Rankscale in the crowded AI monitoring category. Beyond tracking your own citations, the platform shows which content strategies and entity patterns are driving competitor citations, giving you a model to analyze and adapt rather than just a score to improve. This makes it particularly relevant for operators in competitive niches where understanding why a competitor is being cited is as valuable as knowing that they are.
