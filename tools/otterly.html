What Otterly.ai does

Otterly.ai is a dedicated AI search monitoring platform that tracks brand and competitor mentions across six AI engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, and Claude. You configure your brand name, competitor brands, and the specific prompts you want monitored, Otterly runs those prompts on a scheduled cadence and reports back which brands appear, in what position, and with what frequency.

This is pure citation monitoring with no SEO features built in, no keyword rank tracking, no backlink analysis, no content optimization scoring. It does one thing: tells you exactly when and how often AI engines are citing your brand versus competitors. For operators who already have an SEO tool and just need the GEO monitoring layer added, Otterly is the most focused and cost-efficient way to get that data.

The six-engine coverage is the differentiator versus SE Ranking's built-in GEO module, which covers three engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI). For operators who want visibility into Copilot and Claude citation behavior in addition to the big three, Otterly is the only tool in the EAS GEO stack that provides it.

The monitoring workflow

Otterly's setup is straightforward: add your brand, add competitor brands, configure the prompts that represent how your customers search for what you offer. The tool runs those prompts against the configured engines on the schedule you set, daily, weekly, or custom, and returns a citation frequency dashboard showing your brand's visibility trend over time.

The competitor comparison view is the highest-value output for most operators. Seeing which competitors are being cited on the prompts where you're not appearing reveals the specific gaps in your content and entity coverage that are preventing citation. That competitive visibility data is what drives the content calendar decisions for GEO optimization.

Otterly vs SE Ranking's GEO module

The honest comparison: SE Ranking's GEO module is built into a full SEO suite at $65/month. Otterly is a dedicated AI monitoring tool starting at $19/month. If you're already using SE Ranking for keyword tracking and backlinks, you have GEO monitoring built in, Otterly is redundant. If you're using Mangools or Semrush for SEO and need to add AI citation monitoring as a separate layer, Otterly is the most cost-efficient way to get it. The tools serve different use cases and don't directly compete for the same operator scenario.
