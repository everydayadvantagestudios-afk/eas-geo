What SE Ranking does

SE Ranking is a full-suite SEO platform that added native GEO tracking as a core module, not a bolt-on feature, but a first-class tracking view that sits alongside keyword rankings and backlink data in the main dashboard. For a solo operator who wants to track both traditional SEO and AI search visibility from one interface, SE Ranking is currently the only platform that delivers both at the sub-$100/month price point.

The GEO module tracks brand mentions and citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews automatically. You configure the brand name, target queries, and competitor brands, and the module checks those queries on a scheduled basis, reporting back which brands get cited and in what context. This is the AI equivalent of rank tracking, instead of measuring position 1 through 10 in a SERP, it measures whether you appear at all in an AI-generated answer.

The SEO suite covers keyword rank tracking across Google and Bing, backlink monitoring with toxic link detection, on-page SEO auditing, competitor research, and white-label reporting for agencies. For solo operators, the relevant features are keyword tracking, the GEO citation monitor, and the on-page audit, the agency features are there if needed but don't drive the value at the solo tier.

The GEO tracking module in detail

SE Ranking's AI Overviews and GEO tracking module works by running configured prompts against each AI engine on a scheduled cadence and logging the results. The practical output is a citation frequency chart, how often your brand appears in AI answers for the queries you care about, alongside a competitor comparison view showing which brands are appearing instead of yours.

This is the measurement instrument that makes GEO optimization a data-driven practice rather than a guessing game. The HubSpot AI Search Grader gives you a point-in-time baseline. SE Ranking's GEO module gives you a continuous time series. You can see whether the content changes and schema implementations you're making are moving the citation needle, or not, over weeks and months rather than waiting for the next manual grader run.

The competitor comparison view is particularly useful. It shows which brands are being cited in the queries where you're not appearing, which tells you which content and entity patterns you need to match. If Competitor A is consistently cited on queries you're targeting, the SE Ranking output gives you the data to analyze what that competitor is doing that you're not.
