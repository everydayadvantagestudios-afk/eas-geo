What Semrush does

Semrush is the largest SEO and competitive intelligence platform in the market. Its database covers over 25 billion keywords across 140 countries, with backlink data from over 43 trillion links. At the enterprise scale, there is no competitor that matches its depth of competitive research, SERP feature tracking, or content gap analysis.

The GEO capabilities are embedded in two modules. The AI Overviews tracking module (part of Position Tracking) monitors whether your brand and target keywords appear in Google AI Overviews on a daily basis. The Brand Monitoring tool tracks mentions across the web and social platforms, the raw citation signal that feeds into AI engine visibility. Combined, these two modules give you a full picture of both your organic Google AI presence and the third-party citation ecosystem that feeds it.

Semrush also publishes some of the most cited research on AI search trends, the AI Overviews impact studies, the SEO-to-GEO transition data, the citation overlap analysis between Google rankings and AI citations. For operators who want to stay current on how AI search is evolving, Semrush's research content is a primary source.

When Semrush is the right call for a solo operator

Semrush is the right tool when the competitive intelligence at its depth is justified by the scale of the content operation. The specific thresholds where the upgrade makes sense: 10,000+ monthly sessions, publishing six or more articles per month, operating in a competitive niche where knowing exactly which keywords competitors are targeting changes your content calendar. Below those thresholds, SE Ranking and Frase provide sufficient capability at lower cost.

The $139/month Pro plan is the correct entry tier for solo operators. It covers five projects, 500 tracked keywords, and the full suite of competitive research tools. The Guru tier at $249/month adds historical data and content marketing features, relevant at the scale where historical trend analysis changes strategy decisions.

The SEO-to-GEO research advantage

One of Semrush's underrated advantages for GEO strategy is its topic research and content gap analysis, which reveals the specific questions and sub-topics your competitors are covering that you're not. For GEO, this is a citation gap analysis, the topics where other brands are being cited by AI engines that you're not covering in your content. Filling those gaps systematically is how share of voice grows over time.

The competitive positioning for Semrush relative to SE Ranking is honest: Semrush has a larger database, more established brand authority in the SEO research space, and deeper historical data. SE Ranking has a lower price point, native GEO citation tracking on a continuous basis, and European data residency. For operators choosing between them at the early stage: SE Ranking first, Semrush later when the deeper competitive intelligence justifies the cost difference.
